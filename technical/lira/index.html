lira

a piano roll written in c0

lira is a piano roll, a grid of pitches against time where a song is written by clicking notes onto it. it plays the song through oljud and saves it as a noter file. it is an ordinary ring-3 program that start lira.elf starts in a window of its own, as does a click on a song in hylla starts it too.

a first song

type start lira.elf mysong.not in the shell, then press alt-tab or click lira's window to give it the keyboard. rows are pitches and columns are steps, four to a beat and four beats to a bar. the left button puts a note in. the right button takes it away. press space to hear the song and s to save, then type sync in the shell to keep it after a restart. without a file name the song is new. s asks for a name.

the mouse

left button
puts a note in an empty cell. held and moved right, the note grows with it.
right button
takes away the note under it.
a note's ends
drag to make it shorter or longer. the pointer turns to arrows left and right.
a note's middle
drag up or down for how loud it is, or left or right for where it sits between the speakers. the way it moves first decides which. the pointer turns to arrows four ways. a note panned away from the middle has a red mark along its top, where it sits.
the buttons along the bottom
the instrument new notes get, square, triangle, sawtooth or drum.

keys

1 2 3 4
the instrument, as its button does. it is also the one the next three keys act on.
m
mute that instrument, or hear it again. its notes turn dark.
o
solo it, so only it is heard, or hear everything again.
p P
move it a step right or left between the speakers, nine steps in all. the pan is saved with the song, mute and solo are not.
, .
quieter or louder new notes.
- =
the tempo down or up.
arrows, page up, page down
scroll a semitone, a beat or an octave.
home
back to the start of the song.
space
plays the song from the start, or stops it.
l
loop on or off.
s
saves.
h
the help, this list over the grid.
escape
closes the help, or quits lira.

at most eight notes may sound at once. a click that would make a ninth is refused with a message in the status line.
